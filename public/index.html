How to apply these times in Spotify Mix

  1. Open Spotify on your phone and pick the track you want to add.
  2. Long-press the track → Add to Mix.
  3. Tap Edit transitions on the track in the Mix view.
  4. Set Start at to the IN time above and End at to the OUT time above.
  5. Save. Repeat for each track in your mix.
