Party Mode

Turn JioSaavn into the DJ at every Indian party.

A premium feature concept, backed by a working prototype.

Rishi  ·  rishinikamai@gmail.com
The Problem

The “DJ Gap”

Every party song has a peak. The second it ends and the next track starts cold, the dance floor dies and stays dead through the intro.

Chaiyya Chaiyya (7:00)
0:00–0:54
PEAK · 0:54–1:54
1:54 onwards, energy drops
Kajra Re (6:00)
0:00–1:00
PEAK · 1:00–2:20
2:20 onwards
≈ 1 min 6 sec of dead dance floor between two bangers.

Nobody dances to an intro.

The Insight

Every song has a peak.
People only want the peaks.

At a party, listeners want the bangers, back to back, blended smoothly. That is exactly what a DJ does by hand: cut the filler, ride the energy, never let the room cool down.

The peak is already measurable: it’s the most-replayed, highest-energy stretch of the track.

The Solution

Party Mode: one toggle, zero dead air.

  • Flip Party Mode on inside JioSaavn.
  • The app auto-plays each song’s peak section and crossfades straight into the next song’s peak.
  • No requests, no buildup, no buzzkill. Continuous, DJ-style energy.

A DJ in every pocket.

Proof: I already built it

“Groovy”: a working prototype

What it does today

  • Stores each song’s peak (in / out timestamps).
  • Auto-skips the moment the peak ends.
  • Seeks the next song straight to its peak.
  • Schema already built for crowd/AI-derived peaks (confidence scoring).

The wall I hit

It runs on Spotify, and Spotify’s API exposes no crossfade control. So transitions stay glitchy. I can skip and seek, but I can’t blend. I’ve hit a ceiling I cannot break from the outside.

Why JioSaavn, not me, should own this

You can do the one thing
I can’t.

  • JioSaavn owns its own player → full crossfade & gapless control.
  • JioSaavn owns the Indian catalog and the party-culture audience.
  • JioSaavn has no public API, so no third party can build this from outside. Only you can.

You already own the two things Spotify won’t hand me.

The cheap moat

Find the peak without expensive AI.

① Crowdsourced behavior

You already log where users skip, replay, and seek across millions of streams. Aggregate it into a per-song average in-point / out-point. The peak reveals itself.

② No LLM required

Simple statistical aggregation + filtering, with an optional light audio-energy pass. Cheap to compute, and it gets smarter with every stream.

The data already lives on your servers. It’s just never been mined for this.

Why now

Spotify built the mixtape.
Nobody’s built the DJ.

Spotify just shipped “Mix,” but that recreates the sentimental personal mixtape: songs for a loved one. It is not the party, and not the DJ.

The party floor is wide open. First mover owns the category.

The business case

The real market: casual house parties.

Not weddings; those hire a DJ. The everyday apartment, dorm, and pre-game party is where music runs off a phone, and where there's no DJ to save the vibe.

  • Huge & constant: happens every weekend, in every city, with zero booking or budget.
  • High-intent, high-spend moment: people in party mode pay for anything that keeps the night going, and Party Mode is exactly that.
  • Inherently viral: one party exposes a whole room of friends at once → word-of-mouth acquisition.
  • Converts & sticks: a premium reason to upgrade, longer sessions, clean differentiator vs. Spotify & YouTube Music.

Illustrative: lifting paid conversion by just 0.5 pts on a 100M-user base ≈ 500K new subscribers. (Estimate; validate against your real funnel.)

Crowd-derive peaks for the top 10,000 party songs. Pure data work, no player changes.
Phase 2

Phase 3
Party Mode toggle + group / social party sessions (shared queues, multi-room).
